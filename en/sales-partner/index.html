How it works

From code to bank transfer in four steps.

  1. 01

    Open your account

    Sign up with your name, email and country, then verify your email. Once your profile and bank details are complete, your code starting with AOM appears in the portal and in your inbox.

  2. 02

    Show it to a business

    Open the menu on your phone and walk through the plans. The owner signs up to Art of Menu themselves and enters your code before the first payment. A code can't be added afterwards.

  3. 03

    The first payment comes in

    The day the payment reaches us, your commission shows as "pending" in the portal. If there's no refund or cancellation within 30 days, it becomes payable.

  4. 04

    Get paid by transfer

    Once your payable balance passes 1,000 TRY for a bank in Turkey, or €200 or $200 for a bank abroad, we send it by bank transfer.

Questions

Anything not answered here is in the terms.

Partner termsCommission, payouts and every rule
When do I get paid?

A commission becomes payable 30 days after the business's payment arrives. We send a transfer once your balance passes the minimum: 1,000 TRY for a bank in Turkey, €200 or $200 for a bank abroad. Anything below rolls over to the next round.

Do I earn again when the subscription renews?

No. You earn once per business, from its first paid payment. Renewals, branches added later and plan upgrades don't pay commission.

I don't have a company. Can I join?

Yes. Anyone 18 or over can join as an individual; we issue a payment receipt and withhold the tax Turkish law requires. If you join as a company, you invoice us for your commission.

Can I offer the business a discount?

No. Unapproved discounts, gifts or passing part of your commission to the business break the terms. Prices are the same for everyone.

Can I run ads?

You can talk about Art of Menu on your own channels. Search ads on the Art of Menu name or similar words, domains or social accounts using the name, and unsolicited bulk messages are not allowed.

Can I use my code for my own café?

No commission is paid for your own business or any business you co-own or manage. The code is for new businesses you introduce.

Can I sell to businesses in other countries?

Yes. Art of Menu sells in lira in Turkey, in euros in Europe and in dollars elsewhere. Your commission is booked in lira and converted at the Central Bank of Turkey rate on the payment day. Intermediary bank fees on international transfers are yours.

Two partners visited the same business. Who earns?

The partner whose code was entered before the first payment. Each business belongs to one partner, and commission is paid once.
