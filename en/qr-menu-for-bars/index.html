A QR menu for bars that keeps the rounds coming.

A bar QR menu puts your cocktails, beers and bar snacks on every guest's phone. With ArtOfMenu's Order plan, the next round can be ordered from the table without anyone waving for attention.

Get started See how it works
In short

A QR menu for a bar is a drinks and food menu that guests open by scanning a code at their table or on the bar. ArtOfMenu shows photos and allergens in the guest's language. On the Order plan, guests send rounds straight to staff phones. On Smart, recipe-based stock marks a cocktail sold out when one of its ingredients runs out.

Ordering the next round

At 11 pm on a Friday, the hardest part of buying a drink is getting someone's attention. With table ordering, a group picks the next round from the menu and sends it. The order reaches the waiter's phone instantly, with a notification, so it no longer depends on eye contact across a crowded room.

On the Order plan, guests can also call a waiter, ask for water or ask for the bill from the table.

A drinks menu worth scrolling

Cocktail names rarely explain themselves. A photo and one line of ingredients help a guest choose between a mezcal sour and a paloma. List beers by style, show which are on draft and give spirit flights their own section.

Because the menu is live, a new guest tap or a one-night special goes on in a minute and comes off just as quickly.

When a drink runs out

Running out of fresh lime at midnight can knock half your cocktails off the list. With recipe-based stock on the Smart plan, each drink is linked to its ingredients. When one runs out, every drink made with it shows as sold out on its own, and guests stop ordering margaritas you cannot make.

On the Smart plan, sales reports show what moved each night, which makes the next delivery order easier to get right.

Visitors and late-night crowds

Bars near hotels and nightlife streets serve guests from everywhere. The menu opens in the language of each guest's phone, with prices in your local currency, so a group of visitors can order without passing a translation app around the table.

Behind the bar

On the Order plan, every member of staff signs in to the waiter screen with a personal link or QR code, without an email address. Orders and calls from the tables reach their phones in real time, and waiters can mark rounds as delivered or cancel an item.

Frequently asked questions

Can bar guests order drinks from their table with a QR code?

Yes, on ArtOfMenu's Order plan. Guests choose drinks in the menu and send the order, and the waiter sees it instantly on their phone. Payment still happens through your own terminal or POS.

Can guests run a tab through the QR menu?

No. ArtOfMenu does not handle tabs or payments. Guests send orders from the menu, and you keep running tabs and taking payment in your existing POS, the same way you do today.

How do I stop guests ordering drinks we have run out of?

Use recipe-based stock on the Smart plan. Link each drink to its ingredients. When one runs out, every drink that uses it shows as sold out automatically, so nobody orders a mojito after the mint is gone.

Does a QR menu work for a pub?

Yes. Pubs with table service can use it for ordering rounds and food. If guests order at the bar, a QR menu still lets them read the full list of beers and food while they wait their turn.

How much does a QR menu for a bar cost?

The Menu plan is $12 per branch per month. Table ordering and calling a waiter from the table are on Order at $35. Recipe-based stock and sales reports are on Smart at $69.
