A smart QR menu, explained without the buzzwords.

A smart QR menu changes what guests see based on things you would otherwise watch yourself: the weather, what sells together and tomorrow's expected demand. In ArtOfMenu, that comes down to three concrete features.

See how it works
In short

A smart QR menu adjusts itself using information about the day. ArtOfMenu's Smart plan does three things: it reorders the menu when the weather turns cold, suggests the item most often ordered with what a guest just added, and sets tomorrow's prices each evening inside a minimum and maximum the owner chooses. It costs $69 per branch per month.

Menu order that follows the weather

When the temperature drops, hot drinks move up the menu. Cold drinks and items about to run out move down. A guest scanning on a cold morning sees the hot chocolate first, and a guest ordering late in the day is not steered toward a dish with two portions left.

During the day, only the order changes. Prices stay fixed until the next day.

“Goes well with” suggestions

When a guest adds an item, the menu shows the item most often ordered with it at that branch. It learns from your own orders, so it suggests what your guests actually pair: fries with the burger, or the cheesecake your regulars take with an espresso.

It is the suggestion a good waiter makes, offered at every table at the same moment.

Daily smart pricing

Every evening, ArtOfMenu reads the branch location, the weather forecast, holidays and special days, and each item's expected demand. It then sets tomorrow's prices inside the minimum and maximum you have set for each item. Prices change at most once a day, before the day starts.

It also learns from anonymized behavior coefficients of similar businesses. It never uses their prices, and your prices are never shared with anyone.

  • Approve: you see tomorrow's prices and confirm each one
  • Automatic: prices update inside your limits without a step from you
  • Off: the menu uses your base prices
  • Any price you do not approve falls back to the base price

Why the limits matter

Guests notice when a coffee costs far more than it did yesterday. That is why every price stays between a minimum and maximum you choose, and why nothing changes during service. You might let a sandwich move between $9 and $10.50, and keep the house coffee fixed by setting both limits to the same price.

What else the Smart plan includes

Smart includes everything in the Order plan, plus a special-days calendar for pricing, calling a waiter from the table, recipe-based stock that marks items sold out when an ingredient runs out and counter screens that turn pages together. In Europe it costs €59 per branch per month, and yearly billing costs 10 months' price.

Frequently asked questions

What is a smart QR menu?

It is a digital menu that adapts using information about the day instead of staying fixed. In ArtOfMenu it reorders items when the weather turns cold, suggests pairings based on your branch's orders and can set daily prices within limits you choose.

Will prices change while guests are in the restaurant?

No. Prices change at most once a day, before the day starts. During service only the order of items can change, so the price a guest sees at lunch is the price for the whole day.

Is dynamic pricing in restaurants fair to guests?

It depends on how it is done. ArtOfMenu keeps every price inside limits you set and changes prices at most once a day, never during service. You can approve each price yourself, or switch it off and keep your base prices.

Does smart pricing use other restaurants' prices?

No. It learns from anonymized behavior coefficients of similar businesses, never from their prices. Your prices are never shared with other businesses, and theirs are never used to set yours.

Can I turn smart pricing off?

Yes. Choose off and the menu uses your base prices. You can also approve every price by hand, and any price you do not approve falls back to the base price.
