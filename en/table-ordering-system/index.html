A table ordering system that keeps staff on the floor.

A table ordering system lets guests send their order from a QR menu on their phone, so staff receive it without walking over to take it. ArtOfMenu's Order plan sends each order straight to the waiter's phone.

Get started See how it works
In short

A QR table ordering system lets guests scan the code on their table, choose items in the digital menu and send the order. With ArtOfMenu's Order plan, the waiter sees it instantly on a live waiter screen on their phone, with a notification. It works alongside your POS and payment terminal and does not replace them. It costs $35 per branch per month.

What the waiter sees

Orders appear on a live waiter screen on the waiter's phone as they arrive, and notifications mean nobody has to keep checking. The slow part of each visit, waiting to be noticed and then waiting to dictate an order, gets much shorter. A small team can keep up with more tables during the rush.

Calls for a waiter, water or the bill

Some requests are not orders. On the Order plan, guests can call a waiter, ask for water or ask for the bill from the menu, and the request reaches staff on their phones. The table that wants to leave no longer has to wait for someone to walk past.

Who table ordering suits

It fits places where ordering is a bottleneck. It fits less well where ordering is part of the experience, such as a tasting menu a server explains course by course.

  • Lunch spots where every table orders in the same 20 minutes
  • Bars and pubs where getting attention for the next round takes too long
  • Cafés with table service and a small team
  • Places with many visitors who prefer to read and order in their own language

What it does not replace

ArtOfMenu is not a POS and not a payment terminal. Guests send orders; you keep taking payment, printing receipts and running the kitchen the way you do now. If you need card payment at the table, plan for it with your payment provider.

Stock and sales reports

Stock and reports are part of the Smart plan. Recipe-based stock marks items sold out when an ingredient runs out, which stops guests ordering something the kitchen cannot make, and sales reports show what was ordered so you can plan your next purchase.

From scan to plate

  1. Scan

    The guest at table 7 scans the code, and the menu opens in their phone's language.

  2. Choose

    They add a pasta and two glasses of house red, checking the allergens before they decide.

  3. Send

    They send the order, and it appears on the waiter's phone instantly, with a notification.

  4. Serve

    The waiter passes it to the kitchen the way your team already works, then brings the food.

Frequently asked questions

What is a table ordering system?

It is a way for guests to order from their table using a digital menu, usually opened with a QR code. Orders go directly to staff, so the guest does not wait for a waiter to come over and write them down.

Do guests need an app to order from the table?

No. They scan the QR code with the phone camera and order in the browser. The menu opens in the language their phone uses, so visitors can order as easily as your regulars.

Does it replace my POS?

No. ArtOfMenu is not a POS and does not take payments. Orders arrive on the waiter's phone, and your team handles billing, receipts and card payments in the system you already use.

Can guests call a waiter or ask for the bill?

Yes, on the Order plan. Guests can call a waiter, ask for water or ask for the bill from the menu, and staff are notified on their phones. The bill request only tells the waiter; payment happens at the till as usual.

How much does a table ordering system cost?

Table ordering is part of ArtOfMenu's Order plan at $35 per branch per month, or $350 a year with yearly billing, including calls for a waiter, water or the bill. In Europe it costs €29 a month. Smart, at $69, adds daily pricing, recipe-based stock and sales reports.
