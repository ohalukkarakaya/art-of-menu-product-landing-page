In short

QR menu pricing usually depends on features rather than menu size: a menu that only displays items costs less than one that takes orders or adjusts prices. ArtOfMenu has three plans, priced per branch: Menu at $12 a month, Order at $35 and Smart at $69. Yearly billing costs 10 months' price. In Europe the plans cost €9, €29 and €59.

What drives the price of a QR menu

Most QR menu tools charge for what happens after the scan. Showing a menu is the simple part. Taking orders needs a live connection to staff phones and notifications that arrive the moment a guest taps send. Setting prices from weather and demand needs forecasting work every night. That is why plans step up in price as they step up in what they do.

The number of locations is the other factor. Each branch has its own tables, staff and guests, so ArtOfMenu prices each branch separately.

The three plans

All prices below are per branch, per month, in US dollars.

  • Menu, $12: QR menu with photos, allergens, automatic language, your own domain, PDF upload and anonymous statistics.
  • Order, $35: everything in Menu, plus table ordering, a live waiter screen with notifications and simple stock and order reports.
  • Smart, $69: everything in Order, plus daily smart pricing with a special-days calendar, live menu order, “goes well with” suggestions, calling a waiter from the table, recipe-based stock and counter screens.

Yearly billing: 12 months for the price of 10

Paying yearly costs ten times the monthly price. For each branch, Menu comes to $120 a year, Order to $350 and Smart to $690.

Software or printing: an honest comparison

A printed menu has a cost per run, and every price change, new dish or typo means another run. Laminated menus wear, crack and get sticky. Count how many times you reprinted last year, add what each run cost and you have the figure to compare against.

A QR menu has a fixed monthly cost and no reprints. It will not replace every paper copy, and some guests will still ask for one. It pays off most in places that change prices often, run daily specials or serve guests who read other languages.

An affordable QR menu for a small café

The Menu plan is meant for exactly that: one branch, a clear menu with photos and automatic language, at $12 a month. Start there, and move to Order or Smart when table ordering or smart pricing would earn back its cost. If you later move down to a smaller plan, your data stays where it is.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a QR code menu cost?

With ArtOfMenu, a QR code menu costs $12 per branch per month on the Menu plan. Table ordering is on the Order plan at $35, and smart pricing with recipe-based stock is on the Smart plan at $69. Yearly billing costs 10 months' price.

Is there a trial or a plan with no monthly fee?

No. ArtOfMenu has no trial and no unpaid plan. The lowest plan is Menu at $12 per branch per month. You can cancel in one step and keep using it until the end of the period you paid for.

Do I pay per branch or per business?

Per branch. Each location has its own plan price. Two cafés on the Menu plan cost $12 each per month, or $120 each per year with yearly billing. In Europe each would cost €9 a month.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, you can move between plans as your needs change. If you move to a smaller plan, your menu and data stay in place, so nothing has to be rebuilt.

What happens if I cancel?

Cancelling takes one step. Your menu keeps working for guests until the paid period ends, so you are never cut off halfway through a month or year you have already paid for.
